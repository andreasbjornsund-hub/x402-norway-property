How it works

1

Your agent calls a paid endpoint without payment headers.

2

Server responds 402 Payment Required with the price (in USDC) and the payTo address.

3

Agent signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization and re-sends with X-PAYMENT.

4

Facilitator settles on Base. Server returns the data.

Source & freshness

All data comes from Kartverket / Geonorge — addresses, place names, elevation, cadastral. We respect their fair-use policy (10 concurrent max). Address and place caches: 7 days. Elevation: 30 days. Each response carries X-Cache: HIT|MISS.

Coverage: lat 55–81°N, lon –10 to 35°E. Coordinates outside this box are rejected with 400.
